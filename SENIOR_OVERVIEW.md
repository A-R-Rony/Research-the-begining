## 🔬 Inside His Flagship 2026 Paper: The Exact Mechanics
**Paper**: *"A New Attack Surface: XAI-guided Adversarial Comment Generation with LLMs to Attack Fake News Detectors"* (ACM WSDM '26)
- **The Core Problem**: Machine Learning detectors flag fake news on social media by looking not just at the article headline, but at **user comments beneath it**.
- **The Attack**: Malicious actors use an LLM (like Llama) to generate fake user comments that appear completely authentic and deceptive.
- **The XAI Aspect**: They use Explainable AI (feature importance like SHAP/LIME or attention weights) to identify what tokens or tones the detector looks for, then instruct the LLM to generate comments targeting those exact features to make the fake news detector flip its prediction (e.g. classifying fake news as real).
- **The Experimental Dataset**: A curated benchmark of **4,200 samples** (50% adversarial comments, 50% legitimate comments).
